THE CORE IDEA

A mirror of your own kind.

Sometimes, what makes us feel understood is discovering that someone else thinks, feels, creates, explores, or experiences the world in a way that feels familiar.

We show you a mirror of your own kind.

WE ARE. isn't about matching people by superficial similarities. It's about surfacing the dimensions of connection that actually matter — across interests, perspectives, and experience alike.

Talk about what makes you, you.

Start with something you genuinely care about. A conversation can begin with an interest, an idea, an experience, a question, or simply curiosity.

Connection works both ways.

Meaningful connection depends on how everyone shows up. A few principles we ask everyone to hold to:
  • Respect people.
  • Protect your privacy.
  • Share only what you are comfortable sharing.
  • Do not harass or exploit others.
  • Report inappropriate behavior.
  • Keep conversations appropriate for the people involved.

WE ARE. is designed with age-aware experiences in mind, so people can connect in ways appropriate to their age and context.

Connection is bigger than dating.

WE ARE. is built around human connection — friendship, conversation, shared interests, curiosity, community, and the possibility of discovering people who understand a part of you.
